Frequently Asked Questions

1. What types of corporate giveaways do you print? expand_more

We offer a wide range of customized corporate giveaways such as mugs, tumblers, pens, notebooks, keychains, tote bags, plaques, stickers, packaging items, and other promotional products.

2. Can you customize giveaways with our company logo and branding? expand_more

Yes! We can customize your items with your company logo, brand colors, designs, and personalized details to match your corporate identity.

3. What printing method do you use? expand_more

We offer high-quality printing solutions, including Direct UV Printing and other suitable printing methods depending on the product material and design requirements.

4. Do you accept bulk orders? expand_more

Yes, we specialize in bulk corporate orders and can provide special discounts for large quantities.

5. What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)? expand_more

Minimum order requirements vary depending on the product. Contact us with your preferred item and quantity so we can provide the best option for you.

6. How long is the production time? expand_more

Production time depends on the product type, quantity, and design approval. We will provide an estimated timeline once your order details are confirmed.

7. Can you help us choose the best giveaway item for our event? expand_more

Absolutely! We can recommend products based on your budget, event type, target audience, and branding needs.

8. Do you provide design assistance? expand_more

Yes, we can assist with layouts and design suggestions to make sure your logo and artwork look professional on the chosen product.

9. Can we request samples before placing a bulk order? expand_more

Sample availability depends on the product. Feel free to ask us about sample options.

10. How can we place an order? expand_more

Simply send us your preferred product, quantity, size/specifications, and design/logo. Our team will assist you with pricing and order processing.
