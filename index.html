Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order quantity? expand_more

MOQ varies by product. For shirts, it's typically 12 pcs, while for small items like ballpens, it's 50-100 pcs. Contact us for specific item MOQs.

Do you provide digital mockups? expand_more

Yes! Once an order is confirmed, our design team provides digital mockups for your approval before proceeding to full production.

How long is the production lead time? expand_more

Standard production takes 7-10 business days. Rush orders (3-5 days) are available for an additional fee depending on the item.

What file formats do you accept for designs? expand_more

We prefer high-resolution vector files (AI, EPS, SVG) or high-quality PNG/PDF files (at least 300 DPI) for the best print results.
